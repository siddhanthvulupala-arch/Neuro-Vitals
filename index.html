Architectural Rationale: Non-Invasive In-Helmet BCI + Edge Triage

During Artemis lunar south-pole EVAs and Mars transit (where one-way radio latency reaches 4 to 22 minutes), crew members cannot rely on real-time ground flight surgeons during acute hypercapnia, hypoxia, or solar proton events. Neuro Vitals integrates non-invasive dry-electrode prefrontal EEG ($Fp1/Fp2$) and functional Near-Infrared Spectroscopy ($fNIRS$) directly into the helmet headband to quantify cerebral oxygenation ($rSO_2$) and cognitive fatigue ($\frac{\theta+\alpha}{\beta}$) while enabling hands-free P300/Motor-Imagery suit commands.

OSD-570 / Inspiration4 Crew Biometrics

Calibrates continuous orbital heart rate, HRV (RMSSD), SpO₂, sleep architecture, and neurocognitive reaction trajectories across orbital flight phases.

NASA Twins Study & HRP SANS Cohort

Models cephalad fluid shift, retinal nerve fiber layer (RNFL) thickening (µm), optic nerve sheath diameter (ONSD), and urinary NTX bone resorption markers.
